Scans documents with the camera, one page or many. The page is found and straightened by the app, shadows evened out, and its text is read on the phone (Tesseract, seven languages) — nothing is sent anywhere to be read. Each document is a searchable PDF named after the date and hour of capture and its first words, filed in big plain folders. A copy can go to your own WebDAV folder (kDrive, Nextcloud). Black and white, text only.
