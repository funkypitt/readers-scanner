Scans documents with the camera, one page or many. The page is found, straightened and cleaned, a blurred shot is flagged at once, and the text is read on the phone in seven languages. Each document becomes a searchable PDF named after its date and first words, filed in plain folders, shareable as PDF, pictures or text, and copied to your own WebDAV folder (kDrive, Nextcloud) if you like.

At first start you choose the engine: Google ML Kit (most reliable; closed code, needs Google Play services, sends usage statistics to Google, never your documents) or the free ones (the app's own page finder and Tesseract). Black and white, text only.
